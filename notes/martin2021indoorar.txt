Title: Indoor Navigation using Augmented Reality
Author(s): Ashly Martin, John Cheriyan, JJ Ganesh, Joel Sebastian, Jayakrishna V (Amal Jyothi College of Engineering, Kottayam, India)
Group: AR arrow on the camera view (marker-based)
Published: EAI Endorsed Transactions on Creative Technologies 8(26), e1, Feb 2021 (open access, CC BY)


WHAT IT DOES
I read the whole paper. It is a simple marker-based AR navigation app built with Unity and Vuforia. Frame markers (Vuforia offers 512 of them) are stuck on floors or walls at entrances and points of interest; when the user scans one, the app knows where they are and then shows on-screen arrows one by one towards the chosen destination, and the destination can be changed during navigation. Firebase handles user accounts and stored data. The related-work section reviews earlier AR navigation papers, including a comparison of marker types (Vuforia against AprilTag and ArUco, from La Delfa et al., 2015) and the Al Delail et al. 2013 paper that combines image recognition with inertial tracking.


RELEVANT TO MY WORK
It is a minimal example of exactly the UI I want (arrows over the camera view) and of the weakest way to get position: markers placed in the building. That makes it a useful lower bound. The more valuable part for me is the reference list, which points to marker-comparison and AR-navigation papers I can follow up.


USE IT FOR
- Describing the simplest baseline (marker scan, then arrows) in related work.
- Leads: La Delfa et al. (visual marker evaluation, 2015), Mulloni et al. (activity-based AR indoor navigation), Al Delail et al. (2013).
- Practical stack idea: Unity plus an AR SDK plus a cloud database for a quick prototype.


CAVEATS
- There is no quantitative evaluation: the results section is screenshots of the app, so I should not cite it for accuracy or usability claims.
- Markers have to be installed and maintained, and tracking between markers is not described in detail.
- I would treat it as a low-evidence source and cite it only for the design idea.
